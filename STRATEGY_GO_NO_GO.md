# Strategy Go/No-Go

## Decision

**NO-GO for changing the canonical strategy, lowering thresholds, resetting the portfolio, or launching the formal $250,000 experiment.**

## Why

1. The certified ten-year replay contains 296 pre-CIO qualification observations and zero canonical CIO decision observations.
2. Every observation was blocked by missing capability authority.
3. Every observation was crypto and intelligence-only.
4. The current allocatable pilot instruments were not represented.
5. Committee, specialist, CIO, sizing, construction, and implementation alternatives cannot be evaluated from records that never reached those stages.
6. Production persistent-cash and committee/CIO traces have only just been added and need real completed cycles.

## What has been established

- Persistent cash in the certified replay is primarily attributable to pre-CIO capability governance, not demonstrated CIO preference.
- Secondary downside, worst-case, probability, liquidity, and evidence gates are measurable research targets.
- Capability-only shadow selections show both substantial upside observations and material downside tails.
- Lowering the cash hurdle did not change the evaluation-period selection set.
- A strategy change based on this artifact would be overfit to a crypto-only and non-allocatable sample.

## Conditions for a future GO decision

A future strategy review requires:

1. Deployment verification for the current diagnostic release.
2. Production `persistent_cash_diagnostic.v1` and `committee-cio-information-trace.v1` observations across valid completed cycles.
3. Certified historical replay of the actual decision-eligible pilot instruments across multiple regimes.
4. Observations reaching all stages from specialist analysis through implementation.
5. Chronological development/evaluation comparison of the canonical process and shadow variants.
6. Explicit human governance approval.

## Portfolio boundary

The portfolio must not be reset yet. A reset to $250,000 is appropriate only after a strategy version receives approval and a new immutable experiment epoch is intentionally registered.
