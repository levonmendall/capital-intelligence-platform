# Decision Intelligence Completion

## Objective

Complete the information path from world events and market/economic observations to candidate-specific, point-in-time evidence while preserving the existing six-specialist committee, CIO-only investment authority, paper-only execution, fail-closed evidence governance, append-only lineage, construction, and reconciliation.

## Implemented foundation

This release adds five reusable capabilities without adding a strategy or investment authority:

1. **Certified public decision-information bridge** — a strict subset of official/regulatory/issuer/market public records can enter the existing event-forward pipeline only after quality, rights, relevance, materiality, point-in-time, causal, exposure, and market-confirmation gates.
2. **Information capability registry** — distinguishes implemented, configured, credentialed, reachable, collecting, point-in-time capable, historically capable, decision-certified, allocatable, healthy, and degraded information domains.
3. **Asset underwriting matrix** — makes missing asset-specific evidence explicit for equities, fixed income, commodities, FX, crypto, real estate, futures, options, volatility, and alternatives.
4. **Hierarchical macro overlay contract** — supports global/regional growth, inflation, labor, policy, rates, credit, liquidity, fiscal, FX, and commodity evidence in shadow mode until each observation family is certified.
5. **Decision learning and stress contracts** — append-only thesis lifecycle, decision attribution, counterfactual alternatives, source information value, factor decomposition, and deterministic scenario stress testing remain research-only.

The release also adds credential-free research adapters for USAspending, ClinicalTrials.gov, and NIH RePORTER. They preserve current retrieval time and are shadow/research evidence until entity mapping and historical availability are certified.

## Existing capabilities retained

Current production already includes governed event clustering, causal transmission, market confirmation, exposure mapping, cross-sectional opportunity breadth, a price/volume capital-flow proxy, expectations/priced-in analysis, forward research bindings, historical learning, exactly six specialists, CIO-only decisions, portfolio construction, reconciliation, and paper implementation. These capabilities are extended rather than duplicated.

## Certification sequence

Every new evidence family follows:

`collect -> validate -> shadow -> historical replay -> calibration -> decision certify`

No source becomes allocatable merely because it is reachable. Missing data is represented as missing, not neutral. Historical learning and source-value analysis cannot strengthen expected return, confidence, position size, policy, or execution authority automatically.

## Remaining data-depth work

Institutional-depth decision certification still requires source-specific point-in-time histories and validation for global fundamentals, analyst estimates/revisions, full positioning and securities lending, fixed-income terms/default/recovery, commodity physical balances and shipping, on-chain crypto, derivatives surfaces/dealer positioning, and global security-master/corporate-action history. The new capability and underwriting registries make these gaps machine-visible and prevent discovery breadth from being mistaken for underwriting completeness.

## Authority invariants

- Exactly six advisory specialists.
- CIO is the only investment authority.
- No evidence source independently creates or authorizes a portfolio change.
- No threshold is lowered to produce trades.
- Paper-only execution remains enforced.
- Fail-closed point-in-time evidence and append-only lineage remain mandatory.
- Portfolio construction and reconciliation remain independent stages after the CIO initial target.
