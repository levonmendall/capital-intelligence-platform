# Universe, Capability, and Candidate-Reachability Remediation

## Scope

This remediation removes arbitrary ownership boundaries while preserving the canonical strategy, evidence standards, CIO authority, construction controls, paper-only execution, and fail-closed behavior.

## Corrections

1. **Exact active-universe capability authority**
   - The current certified active-universe publication is the instrument-level authority used by screening, opportunity qualification, committee review, construction, and paper execution.
   - The original 15 listed instruments remain a bootstrap and regression baseline, not an exclusive whitelist.
   - A classified liquid public-market instrument is eligible when its exact identity and complete analytical, execution, custody, settlement, currency, lifecycle, and risk capability record are current and approved.

2. **Universal point-in-time authority wiring**
   - Production opportunity engines load the same exact active-universe capability authority used by execution.
   - Missing, stale, mismatched, or malformed active-universe publications fail closed; the runtime cannot silently fall back to a narrower static list.
   - Historical replay remains research-only where current capability records did not exist at the historical cutoff.

3. **Complete certified-universe screening**
   - Candidate-count, shortlist, catalog-order, and committee-attention quotas do not remove otherwise qualified assets.
   - Provider catalogs must be complete or explicitly certified as complete. A provider response that reaches its completeness sentinel is rejected rather than silently truncated.
   - Built-in exchange, futures-root, and option-underlying configuration is bootstrap coverage; a certified provider-neutral catalog may add any classified instrument and venue.

4. **Capability-based instrument structures**
   - Asset-class and instrument-type code whitelists are not ownership authorities.
   - The active capability publication identifies the instrument structure and required models. Unknown or new structures remain fail-closed until those capabilities are supplied, rather than requiring a permanent product-scope exclusion.
   - Foreign-currency bonds and other non-USD instruments may participate when currency translation, settlement, liquidity, and risk evidence are complete.

5. **Asset-specific evidence applicability**
   - Value, momentum, carry, and improving-conditions factors must be substantively scored when applicable.
   - A factor may be explicitly not applicable only with point-in-time provider lineage, methodology, evidence identifiers, and rationale.
   - Missing evidence is never converted into a neutral score or an unsupported not-applicable declaration.

6. **Portfolio construction without a fixed search bottleneck**
   - Small approved sets receive exact combinatorial evaluation.
   - Larger sets use a dynamically expanding, governed state budget rather than a fixed four-state beam.
   - Portfolio survival, cash, liquidity, cost, turnover, concentration, correlation, currency, leverage, derivative lifecycle, and downside controls remain binding.

## Production-path invariant

Every candidate supplied to formal qualification is represented as qualified or rejected with explicit reasons. Every qualified candidate receives six independent specialist analyses and a CIO decision before construction. Construction may reduce an approved target or leave it at zero, but it cannot erase the candidate's committee and CIO consideration record.

## Authority boundary

- Canonical investment strategy changed: **no**
- Investment thresholds lowered: **no**
- CIO or specialist authority expanded: **no**
- Portfolio-risk controls removed: **no**
- Paper-only boundary changed: **no**
- Real-money authority added: **no**
- Historical policy promotion authorized: **no**
