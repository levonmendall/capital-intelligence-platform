# Portfolio-First Interface Redesign

## Objective

The interface now explains markets, economic conditions, and daily events through one consistent question: **what does this mean for the governed portfolio?** The presentation remains educational and plain-language while preserving the CIO process as the only authority for portfolio conclusions and paper implementation.

## Today surface

The daily information block is now organized as an investment-learning chain:

1. **What changed** — the few material daily developments.
2. **Why investors care** — the return, risk, rate, liquidity, or earnings channel involved.
3. **Portfolio effect** — the current implication for the governed portfolio.
4. **CIO response** — the portfolio action or deliberate inaction.
5. **What to watch next** — the evidence that could confirm or weaken the interpretation.

Full developments, source attribution, timestamps, and audit references remain available in collapsed detail.

## Environment surface

The economic briefing now presents the core readings most relevant to investing:

- Inflation
- Unemployment
- Federal funds rate
- 10-year minus 2-year Treasury spread

The synopsis explains how growth reaches earnings, inflation reaches purchasing power and margins, and policy rates reach financing costs and discount rates. Economic evidence remains an input to governed comparison rather than a standalone trade signal.

## History surface

Operating reports, historical learning, and report archives now use the same compact card language as the primary surfaces. Long replay tables and execution-attempt records are collapsed by default. Historical evidence is explicitly framed as research and governance input that may inform review or reduce confidence and size, but cannot authorize execution or override the live CIO process.

## Visual system

- Permanent dark appearance
- Cleaner segmented navigation
- Tighter mobile spacing and card geometry
- More restrained gradients, grid texture, and glow
- Improved muted-text contrast
- Consistent portfolio-lens panels across daily and economic content
- Compact two-column metric layouts on mobile where appropriate
- Technical detail remains accessible without dominating the default experience

## Authority boundary

These changes affect presentation and educational interpretation only. They do not alter candidate qualification, CIO authority, construction, execution controls, governance, or real-money permissions.
