# Shadow Variant Comparison

## Governance boundary

All variants are research-only. They use a chronological 70% development / 30% evaluation split. They do not compound a portfolio, authorize construction or execution, promote policy, or support performance claims.

Because the certified replay contains no CIO decisions, only pre-CIO capability and qualification counterfactuals are identifiable.

## Identifiable variants

The evaluator tested:

1. Capability-certified continuous ranking
2. Capability-certified 1% starter positions
3. Capability-certified graduated sizing
4. Capability certification plus a downside/worst-case pair relaxation
5. A 50-basis-point lower cash hurdle

Each cutoff selects at most one candidate. The selection must have a positive opportunity edge, except for the explicit cash-hurdle variant.

### Evaluation-period results

| Variant | Selected cutoffs | Resolved | Positive excess | Negative excess | Median excess | 10th percentile excess | Maximum weight |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Continuous ranking | 27 | 22 | 14 | 8 | 20.98% | -26.27% | 1.00% |
| Starter position | 27 | 22 | 14 | 8 | 20.98% | -26.27% | 1.00% |
| Graduated sizing | 27 | 22 | 14 | 8 | 20.98% | -26.27% | 3.80% |
| Downside-pair relaxation | 27 | 22 | 14 | 8 | 20.98% | -26.27% | 1.00% |
| Lower cash hurdle | 27 | 22 | 14 | 8 | 20.98% | -26.27% | 1.00% |

The identical evaluation selections show that, in this sample, relaxing the cash hurdle or the paired downside controls did not add evaluation-period candidates beyond capability-only observations. Graduated sizing only increased hypothetical exposure to the same crypto selections.

The evaluation sample still had a materially negative lower tail. These results are unsuitable for strategy promotion because the replay is crypto-only, capability-blocked, and does not reproduce the current pilot universe.

## Non-identifiable variants

The following requested variants remain not evaluable:

- reliability-weighted specialist evidence;
- explicit specialist-disagreement handling;
- portfolio marginal-contribution analysis;
- alternative CIO synthesis.

No replay observation reached those stages.

## Conclusion

The shadow results support further study of small, governed initial positions **after proper capability certification**, but they do not justify enabling crypto, changing the canonical strategy, lowering thresholds, or resetting the portfolio.
