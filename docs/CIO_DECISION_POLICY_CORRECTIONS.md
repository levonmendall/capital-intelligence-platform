# CIO Decision Policy Corrections

This change intentionally excludes the governed candidate and holding evidence publishers, production-context adapters, and decision-memory restoration completed in PR #226.

## Implemented

- Evidence vetoes carry a governed consequence category.
- A pure operational evidence outage freezes increases but does not force an immediate holding sale.
- Material uncertainty can reduce a holding only through normal persistence and cooldown controls.
- A thesis contradiction or evidence-integrity emergency may bypass hysteresis for immediate de-risking.
- Specialist-packet persistence records the category beside every veto reason.
- Opportunity ranking uses the active versioned construction minimum-cash policy instead of a separate hard-coded reserve.

## Existing controls retained rather than duplicated

- Joint portfolio scenario construction and multi-start allocation selection.
- Dependency-aware evidence-origin reconciliation and confidence limits.
- Champion-challenger policy promotion and rollback governance.
- Candidate and holding evidence publication and prior-decision/thesis restoration from PR #226.

## Future empirical work

Policy thresholds should remain frozen during the initial paper test, then be evaluated through the existing champion-challenger governance using calibration, drawdown, turnover, missed-opportunity, and regime-coverage evidence. No automatic policy promotion is authorized.
