# Phase 1 — Economic Qualification Consistency

## Purpose

Make exploration and participation research lanes non-authoritative for portfolio capital until the candidate clears the same canonical economic requirements that govern every other positive allocation.

## Required behavior

A candidate may continue through specialist and CIO research review when it is analytically interesting but below full economic qualification. However, BUY or INCREASE authority requires all of the following:

- complete applicable evidence;
- evidence-adjusted return at or above the resolved policy profile;
- probability of outperforming the governing alternative at or above policy;
- robust opportunity edge at or above policy;
- positive stressed edge after an adverse probability shift;
- acceptable downside and probability of loss;
- valid funding and implementation;
- positive supported weight under every robustness control.

## Preserved behavior

- Current holdings still receive mandatory review even when acquisition standards fail.
- Research lanes still reach the six specialists and CIO when hard evidence, identity, liquidity, downside, cost and integrity controls pass.
- Exploration may still use a smaller cap, shorter persistence and closer monitoring after it clears the canonical economic gates.
- No threshold is lowered.
- No trade is forced.
- Portfolio construction remains an independent reduction and survival authority.
- Execution remains paper only.

## Acceptance criteria

- `allow_soft_failures` is never enabled by the CIO allocation path.
- Progressive lanes cannot bypass absolute return, probability, robust-edge or stressed-edge requirements.
- A below-hurdle progressive candidate produces WATCH or NO_SUPERIOR_OPPORTUNITY with no positive target.
- The opportunity record identifies progressive lanes as non-authoritative research lanes.
- Existing full-conviction, holding-review, reduction and exit behavior remains covered by regression tests.
