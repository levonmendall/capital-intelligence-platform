# Compounding Mandate

> **Every recommendation is compared against all other available uses of capital, implemented at the portfolio level, continuously monitored against an explicit thesis, and evaluated afterward using the exact evidence available when the decision was made.**

The platform has one investment mandate: maximize long-term compounded portfolio returns after implementation costs and within approved operational constraints.

## Not investment objectives

Preservation, income, balanced, growth, tactical, value, global, and innovation are not active mandates. They may describe evidence, exposures, or historical research, but they cannot create separate decision authorities or competing portfolio goals.

## Operational constraint profiles

Constraint profiles may specify minimum cash, maximum position and sector exposure, factor and correlation limits, liquidity participation, turnover, transaction-cost limits, leverage prohibitions, and restricted instruments. These profiles govern implementation feasibility only. They do not change opportunity ranking or the CIO objective.

## Compatibility boundary

The retired mandate/trading database may retain historical labels for migration audit. Active portfolio state, scheduling, alerts, construction, and paper execution use the canonical compounding portfolio authority.
