# Market benchmark comparison

The Portfolio surface compares the canonical governed paper portfolio with six familiar market benchmark ETFs over the same recorded evaluation window:

- SPY — S&P 500
- QQQ — Nasdaq-100
- VTI — Total U.S. stock market
- VT — Total world equities
- AGG — U.S. aggregate bonds
- SGOV — 0–3 month U.S. Treasuries

Benchmark returns are read from adjusted daily Alpaca/IEX bars and are withheld rather than estimated when the same-window data is unavailable. The canonical paper portfolio return continues to come from immutable paper-operation evidence.

These benchmarks are measurement yardsticks only. They cannot nominate assets, change qualification or cash hurdles, alter thresholds, affect ranking, sizing or construction, authorize execution, create a second investment objective, or authorize real money. The frozen 80% VTI / 20% SGOV benchmark in the formal paper-experiment protocol is unchanged; that research protocol benchmark is separate from this user-facing market comparison set.
