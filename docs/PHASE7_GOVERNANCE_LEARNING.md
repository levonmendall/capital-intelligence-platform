# Phase 7 — Canonical Policy, Evidence-Outage Aging, and Advisory Learning

Phase 7 gives opportunity screening and CIO qualification one deterministic, fingerprinted policy authority and rejects policy drift when components do not reference the same authority.

Existing holdings now receive bounded operational evidence-outage treatment based on asset risk, outage age, confidence decay, independent substitute evidence, and continued custody, settlement, and lifecycle observability. A short operational outage may preserve a holding with reduced confidence; an outage beyond its governed window escalates to reduction; lost custody, settlement, or lifecycle observability requires immediate risk reduction.

Historical evaluation remains advisory. It reports forecast error, downside breaches, scenario coverage, false positives, missed opportunities, avoided losses, and gate contribution across governed segments. It cannot automatically change policy, lower thresholds, authorize capital, construct a portfolio, or execute a trade.

Exactly six specialists, CIO-only investment authority, fail-closed evidence, independent portfolio construction, append-only point-in-time lineage, and paper-only execution remain unchanged.
