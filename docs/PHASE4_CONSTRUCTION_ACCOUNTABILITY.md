# Phase 4 — CIO-to-Construction Accountability

Every CIO decision now receives an immutable reconciliation record comparing the CIO target with the final portfolio-construction target. The record distinguishes exact implementation, construction reduction, an approved allocation reduced to zero, partial risk reduction, non-action and missing construction. It also preserves binding constraints, funding conflict and displacement by competing allocations.

Focused construction, canonical-cycle and append-only journal regressions passed during deterministic materialization. Full repository and security validation remain authoritative before merge.
