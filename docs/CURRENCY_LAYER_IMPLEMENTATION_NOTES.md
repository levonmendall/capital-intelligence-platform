# Currency Layer Implementation Notes

The dedicated currency and global-liquidity layer is part of PR 10 in the Committee and CIO Decision-System V2 sequence.

It must distinguish the cause of dollar strength before translating it into asset effects. Dollar appreciation driven by stronger U.S. growth, wider real-rate differentials, Federal Reserve tightening, safe-haven demand, foreign weakness or dollar-funding stress cannot share one hard-coded conclusion.

The layer will evaluate trade-weighted and bilateral exchange rates, real effective valuation, rate differentials, carry, forwards, options, cross-currency basis, hedging costs, intervention risk and reserve/collateral demand. It will translate those conditions into effects on other currencies, emerging-market debt and equities, commodities, multinational earnings, international hedged and unhedged returns, U.S. inflation, gold, crypto, credit and capital flows.

Currency evidence will feed the existing six specialists and CIO. It will not independently nominate a positive portfolio target, size a position, construct the portfolio, authorize execution or alter the paper-only boundary.
