# Phase 2 — Applicable Evidence and Valuation Completeness

This document defines the second ordered correction under issue #347.

Every new or increased exposure must include the applicable business, valuation or return-driver evidence for its economic exposure and instrument structure. A required specialist packet may be neutral, but it cannot be absent and treated as an ordinary abstention.

The implementation preserves holding review and allows a current holding to be reduced or exited when evidence deteriorates. It does not lower return, risk, liquidity or opportunity thresholds; add live-money authority; or add a seventh specialist.
