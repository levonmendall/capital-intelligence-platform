# Phase 2 — Applicable Evidence and Valuation Completeness

This document is staged on the Phase 2 branch and defines the next ordered correction under issue #347.

Every new or increased exposure must include the applicable business, valuation or return-driver evidence for its economic exposure and instrument structure. A required specialist packet may be neutral, but it cannot be absent and treated as an ordinary abstention.

The implementation will preserve holding review and allow a current holding to be reduced or exited when evidence deteriorates. It will not lower return, risk, liquidity or opportunity thresholds; add live-money authority; or add a seventh specialist.
