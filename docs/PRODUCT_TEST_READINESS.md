# Product Test Readiness

Product-readiness reports are diagnostics, not launch clearance. Paper testing can
begin without an immutable baseline, burn-in, recovery campaign, launch report,
eligibility package, human release decision, or active runtime switch.

The diagnostic still reports whether market data, eligible instruments, portfolio
construction, paper execution, product surfaces, security, and operational evidence
are healthy. Missing diagnostic evidence may narrow or block a specific instrument
or transaction through its own technical control, but it does not impose a separate
pre-launch waiting period.

No readiness state authorizes real money or performance claims.
