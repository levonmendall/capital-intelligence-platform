# CIO decision-quality corrections

The canonical CIO process now corrects four production decision-quality gaps while preserving paper-only, fail-closed authority.

## Economic exposure controls

Listed wrappers retain their execution class, but decision policy also resolves the underlying economic exposure. The strictest requirements across wrapper structure, economic exposure, derivative use, and decision horizon govern return hurdles, probability, downside, persistence, robustness, and position size. Current persisted pilot wrappers receive a versioned compatibility classification until refreshed candidate records replace them.

## Historical authority separation

Pre-CIO opportunity-qualification outcomes and completed CIO decisions are separate evidence channels. Qualification observations remain visible for admission and abstention research, but they cannot alter CIO action, confidence, or position size. CIO confidence and sizing may be conservatively calibrated only from comparable completed CIO decision outcomes. If no suitable CIO outcome sample exists, those controls remain unmodified.

## Evidence-derived pilot priors

The listed-wrapper pilot derives scenario probabilities, evidence dimensions, forecast quality, valuation confidence, and implementation-cost assumptions from disclosed point-in-time history, freshness, liquidity, volatility, and observed distribution consistency. These remain versioned pilot priors rather than institutional calibration or performance claims.

Cross-sectional breadth and independent positioning are explicitly unavailable in the free IEX evidence path. Their numeric contract placeholders are not treated as neutral evidence, and specialist confidence is constrained accordingly. All four collected FRED inputs—long yield, curve shape, policy rate, and volatility—participate in macro classification.

## Comparable capital alternatives

Cash, current holdings, and competing candidates are compared through a conservative evidence- and liquidity-adjusted return estimate before candidate-horizon robustness controls are applied. Raw expected return alone cannot make a weakly supported alternative the governing use of capital.

## Safety boundary

These corrections do not authorize brokerage submission, real-money activity, policy promotion, or performance claims. Final allocation remains subject to evidence vetoes, CIO synthesis, robust sizing, portfolio construction, and paper-execution governance.

Repository release validation remains mandatory before this decision-quality version may be merged or deployed.
