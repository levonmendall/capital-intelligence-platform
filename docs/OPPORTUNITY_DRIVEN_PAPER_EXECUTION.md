# Opportunity-Driven Paper Execution

## Governing invariant

Capital Intelligence paper operation is opportunity-driven rather than schedule-limited.

Every distinct governed opportunity recognized by the active intelligence and monitoring stack may request a timely canonical CIO evaluation. If the CIO authorizes a nonzero portfolio change and construction remains feasible, the existing autonomous paper executor may attempt the exact reconciled construction immediately.

A recent review of one opportunity must not suppress a different opportunity solely because both occur inside the same wall-clock interval.

## What changed

The historical event cooldown is retained only as a **same-opportunity deduplication lifetime**. Price conditions, public-evidence records, declared reactive dependencies, and cross-market leadership conditions receive stable opportunity keys. A previously claimed key is deduplicated; an unrelated new key remains immediately eligible for a triggered CIO cycle.

Scheduled CIO cycles remain comprehensive full-market sweeps and operational backstops. They do not own exclusive investment-decision timing, and the default event path no longer imposes a pre/post scheduled-cycle guard.

The canonical opportunity reassessment loop is capped at a one-minute scan interval even when an older deployment setting specifies a slower materiality scan. This is a discovery fallback, not an independent trade trigger.

## Authority boundaries retained

Opportunity detection still has no portfolio authority. The following remain mandatory and fail closed where applicable:

- point-in-time evidence and freshness
- six specialist analyses and visible dissent
- CIO-only investment authority
- cash hurdle and expected-return qualification
- portfolio construction and sizing
- position, concentration, correlation, liquidity, turnover, cost, and drawdown constraints
- exact instrument paper-capability certification
- market-session and execution-model validity
- custody, settlement, lifecycle, accounting, and reconciliation simulation
- exact construction authorization and execution idempotency
- canonical portfolio reconciliation

No change authorizes live money.

## Shadow opportunity learning

Qualified/capability-observed instruments that are currently `research_only` or `suspended` at the production capability boundary are written to an append-only shadow opportunity ledger.

The shadow ledger exists to preserve learning about opportunities that cannot safely create or increase canonical paper exposure. It has no CIO authority, construction authority, execution authority, or canonical portfolio mutation path. A shadow observation can never be promoted into a fill merely because it was recorded.

## Failure and retry behavior

Triggered CIO cycles keep distinct durable cycle keys. If an event cycle fails, only the opportunity claims attached to that failed trigger are released. Other recently evaluated opportunities remain deduplicated, while the failed opportunity can be retried promptly once the blocking condition clears.

## Paper-system purpose

The paper system should permit the investment process to express every opportunity it can govern truthfully. Paper status removes financial risk; it does not remove the need for realistic evidence, portfolio, execution, and accounting controls. The system therefore maximizes experimentation by removing arbitrary timing suppression while preserving the controls required for meaningful learning.
