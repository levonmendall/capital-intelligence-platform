# Phase 6 — Candidate and Joint Portfolio Risk Intelligence

The existing Portfolio & Risk specialist now receives candidate-level conditional loss, expected shortfall, time-underwater, recovery, stress-liquidity, execution-cost, thesis-fragility and opportunity-half-life diagnostics. Candidate pairs are also classified as complementary, competing, dominated, basket candidates or independent using factor similarity, correlation buckets, tail dependence and risk-adjusted economics.

Ordinary uncertainty remains a sizing and monitoring input. Only genuine stress-liquidity or jointly severe tail-survival failures create implementation blocks. Exactly six specialists, CIO-only authority, existing economic hurdles, independent construction and paper-only execution remain unchanged.

This phase is intentionally non-authoritative: its outputs enrich the existing Portfolio & Risk specialist and remain subordinate to evidence completeness, robust economic edge, downside, liquidity, cost, CIO qualification, independent construction, and paper-only implementation controls.
