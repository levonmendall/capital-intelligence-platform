# Return Opportunity Discovery

## Objective

The CIO must compare cash with the strongest observable uses of capital rather than treating abstention as success. The production paper cycle therefore preserves strategic cross-asset allocation while adding broad U.S.-company discovery and company-specific evidence.

## Daily sequence

1. Read the full active Alpaca U.S.-equity asset list.
2. Intersect it with the official SEC ticker and exchange master.
3. Require active, tradable, fractionable securities on approved U.S. venues and exclude obvious funds, acquisition vehicles, units, and warrants.
4. Read batched IEX snapshots across the eligible list and apply minimum price and dollar-volume requirements.
5. Deepen the strongest current movers and liquid names, plus every current or unresolved tracked holding, with daily price history.
6. Rank companies using multi-horizon momentum, VTI-relative strength, consistency, liquidity, volatility, and drawdown.
7. Fetch public SEC annual facts for the selected companies and build company-specific quality, growth, profitability, balance-sheet, valuation, momentum, and risk evidence.
8. Submit complete candidates and explicit exclusions to the existing Opportunity Engine, specialist committee, CIO, construction engine, and paper executor.

## Graduated allocation

A new company can receive at most a 1% exploratory paper target. This lane uses a modestly lower opportunity-edge threshold because the position-level loss is tightly capped, but it still requires complete evidence, a positive stressed edge, acceptable downside, a funding source, feasible construction, and current execution evidence. Scaling beyond the exploratory position requires later cycles, the standard company policy, and the normal persistence controls.

## Opportunity-cost accountability

The append-only screening-outcome ledger records qualified and rejected candidates. After at least 21 days, later governed prices are compared with the contemporaneous cash return. The system reports missed opportunities, avoided losses, supported gains, and supported losses without rewriting the original decision.

## Boundaries

- Paper-only; real money remains unauthorized.
- Discovery has no CIO, sizing, construction, or execution authority.
- Strategic cross-asset wrappers remain in the opportunity set.
- Direct non-U.S. listings, options, futures, bonds, spot FX, direct crypto, leverage, and private assets remain outside this paper execution lane.
- The free-data lane does not yet include proprietary analyst revisions, institutional flows, or consolidated full-market depth.
