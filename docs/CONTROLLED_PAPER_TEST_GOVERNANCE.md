# Optional Certified Paper Campaign

The process-freeze, eligibility-package, independent-validation, human-release,
launch-report, and runtime-control authorities are optional audit mechanisms. They
are not prerequisites for ordinary paper testing.

A team may voluntarily use them when it needs a formally frozen cohort for external
review, model governance, or institutional evidence. Such a campaign remains
paper-only and cannot authorize real money.

The application execution path instead relies on current technical controls, exact
user consent, provider and instrument eligibility, portfolio integrity, safety
limits, and reconciliation.
