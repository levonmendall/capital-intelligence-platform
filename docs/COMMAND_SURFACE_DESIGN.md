# Command surface design

The user experience keeps one consistent Capital Intelligence design system while giving each primary page a distinct purpose, visual rhythm, and interaction model.

## Today

**Purpose:** answer what deserves attention now.

The Today surface uses a decision-pulse identity. It emphasizes one governed conclusion, what changed, why it matters, and the current capital posture. Continuous market analysis remains visually secondary until a material change earns attention.

## Environment

**Purpose:** explain the conditions shaping portfolio decisions.

The Environment surface uses a market-atmosphere identity. Growth, inflation, liquidity, policy, and cross-asset evidence appear as a connected signal field rather than as another decision page. Environment evidence informs decisions but cannot act independently.

## Portfolio

**Purpose:** show how capital is positioned and how a decision would be implemented.

The Portfolio surface uses a capital-architecture identity. It follows a simple sequence: size, fund, validate. Allocation, cash optionality, construction status, implementation cost, positions, and capital history are presented as one coherent capital map.

## History

**Purpose:** connect decisions with outcomes and learning.

The History surface uses an institutional-memory identity. A chronological activity rail connects the latest decision, outcome, thesis state, and paper execution before the user enters detailed records.

## Shared constraints

All four surfaces retain the same navigation, data authority, terminology, dark/light appearance control, portfolio mandate, and governance boundaries. Distinction comes from hierarchy, visual identity, and information rhythm—not additional screens, controls, or complexity.
