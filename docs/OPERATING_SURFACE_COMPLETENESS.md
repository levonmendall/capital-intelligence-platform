# Operating surface completeness

The persistent Render application distinguishes operating evidence, governed decisions, implementation, and historical research without inventing missing authority.

## Live environment

When the legacy diagnostic regime snapshot is absent but Alpaca/IEX and FRED are available, the Environment surface presents the provider-backed evidence field as active. It does not synthesize a regime label or treat the evidence as an independent recommendation.

## No-action CIO decisions

A governed no-action briefing retains its canonical briefing identifier as the decision reference when no candidate-specific decision identifier exists. Unscored confidence is displayed as `Not scored` with a no-candidate explanation rather than as a blank value. No-action implementation metrics are shown as unchanged, zero turnover, and not applicable where no construction is required.

## Historical certification

Historical calibration remains blocked when point-in-time macro values were unavailable at required decision cutoffs, even when all required series exist somewhere in the archive. The interface reports both facts explicitly and does not bypass certification.

## Mobile presentation

Long decision states and native Streamlit metric values wrap on narrow screens instead of being silently truncated.

These changes preserve paper-only operation and do not authorize real-money execution, force a transaction, or use historical research as live execution authority.
